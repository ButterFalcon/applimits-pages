Troubleshooting

My restrictions are not working properly

Some iOS 26 versions could enforce usage limits too early. This issue has improved in iOS and iPadOS 27.2.1. Update to 27.2.1 or later in Settings → General → Software Update. If 27.2.1 is unavailable, install the latest update offered for your device.

How to update your iPhone or iPad

If the issue continues, check the selected apps, days, hours, and Disable for Today setting. On iPhone or iPad, allow Applimits to access Screen Time. If you use Share Across Devices, try temporarily turning it off on the affected device, then set up the usage limit again. On Android, check Usage Access and verify that the Applimits accessibility service is enabled.

Restrictions stopped working after an OS update

Restart your device, open Applimits, and check that permissions are enabled. Open the affected restriction, review the selected apps, days, and times, then save it again.

How do I report a problem with restrictions?

In Applimits, go to Settings → Contact. Include your device model, OS and Applimits versions, steps to reproduce the problem, and a screenshot of your usage limit or scheduled restriction settings.

(iPhone/iPad) Can I prevent the Applimits app from being deleted?

In Applimits, go to Settings → Uninstall Protection for instructions for your OS. On iOS and iPadOS 27 or later, use Screen Time → Passcodes & Management to lock Screen Time settings with a passcode. On earlier versions, restricting app deletion applies to the entire device.

Getting Started

What is Applimits?

Applimits helps you track app usage, set daily usage limits, block apps during scheduled hours, and temporarily block selected apps while you focus.

How do I get started?

Open the app and follow the prompts to enable permissions. On iPhone or iPad, allow Screen Time access. On Android, check Usage Access and enable the Applimits accessibility service.

Add a restriction under Usage Limits, choose the apps and days, set the daily limits, and save.

How do usage limits differ from scheduled restrictions?

A usage limit caps daily time spent on selected items. For example, allow up to 30 minutes of social media on weekdays.

A scheduled restriction blocks selected items during set hours. For example, block social media from 10 p.m. to 7 a.m.

What is the difference between the free version and the Pro plan?

The free version supports one usage limit. Applimits Pro supports up to six and includes features such as Hard Restriction.

Check the purchase screen for pricing and any available trial terms.

Usage Time Limit

How do I set usage time limits?

In Usage Limits, select the apps and set a limit for each day. When a single limit includes several apps, their usage time is combined.

What is "Disable for Today"?

The "Disable for Today" feature lets you remove today's restriction and set it to start from the next day.

Use case:

  • You need to use an app today for special reasons but want to restrict it from tomorrow
What happens when I reach the time limit?

When you reach the limit, a block screen appears when you open a restricted app. The allowance resets at midnight and follows the settings for the new day. When available, Disable for Today temporarily pauses that day's restriction.

(iPhone/iPad) Which apps are affected when restricting by category?

The restriction applies to apps in the category selected through Apple Screen Time. To restrict specific apps, select them individually.

Scheduled Restriction

When is Scheduled Restriction most effective?

This feature completely blocks apps during specific time periods. It is effective in situations such as:

Bedtime: Block social media from 10:00 PM to 7:00 AM to improve sleep quality

Study time: Block games and video apps from 7:00 PM to 9:00 PM

Late night prevention: Block entertainment apps from 0:00 AM to 6:00 AM to prevent staying up late

Can Scheduled Restriction span across midnight?

Yes. For example, a schedule from 10 p.m. to 7 a.m. applies from 10 p.m. on the start day until 7 a.m. the next morning.

Focus timer

What is the Focus timer?

The Focus timer immediately restricts selected apps for a set period. Choose the apps and how long you want to focus, then start the timer. Choose Pomodoro to alternate focus and breaks.

Will I receive a notification when the timer ends?

Turn on notifications in the timer settings and allow notifications for Applimits in your device settings to receive an alert when the timer ends.

Hard Restriction

What is Hard Restriction?

Hard Restriction is an Applimits Pro feature that limits changes to usage limits and scheduled restrictions. You can choose a countdown with an end date and time. The timer also has an option that prevents stopping it while it runs.

When can I edit my settings again after using Hard Restriction?

It depends on the mode and settings. A countdown unlocks settings at the specified end date and time. If you switch it off early, editing may remain locked until the time shown in the app, such as midnight.
